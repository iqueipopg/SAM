01

Introduction & objective

Systematic reviews and meta-analyses are the gold standard of evidence synthesis, yet they are slow and expensive: on average 12.5 months per review (Borah et al., 2017), and large reviews can exceed $300,000.

Our objective is not merely to test a pipeline, but to build an open, free tool that saves thousands of hours and euros while delivering results at least as good as human reviewers.

We built a 9-agent system that mirrors the standard meta-analysis workflow, and benchmarked it against human expert decisions using two language models: an open model (Qwen 2.5 7B) and a frontier model (Claude Opus).

02

Methodology

Validated against a real, published gold standard: a meta-analysis on physical-activity promotion (Collazo-Castiñeira et al., 2025), with full lists of included / excluded studies and exclusion reasons.

  • Each agent reproduces one stage of the standard meta-analysis pipeline.
  • Agreement with human expert decisions is measured with Cohen's κ, sensitivity and specificity.
  • Matched instructions across both models for a fair open-vs-frontier comparison.
Cohen's κ measures agreement beyond chance: 1 = perfect, 0 = chance-level. For ordinal risk-of-bias ratings we use quadratic κ, which penalizes large disagreements more than small ones.

Explainability

A9 · every decision is traceable

SAM is not a black box. Every decision is logged with its justification in an explainability report and a paper-by-paper spreadsheet (agent A9), so each inclusion / exclusion is fully traceable.

Key takeaway

At full-text screening, SAM with a frontier model reaches κ = 0.62 — substantial agreement with the human expert, comparable to typical human inter-reviewer agreement in screening tasks.

The open model reaches reliable agreement at abstract screening but not at full text.

07 · ConclusionAccelerating evidence synthesis without giving up expert judgment

SAM automates the full systematic-review cycle and reproduces expert decisions with substantial agreement, while keeping every step traceable. An open, free tool to accelerate evidence synthesis without giving up expert-level judgment.

08 · Limitations & future work

Single-domain validation (physical activity). Single run per condition — no significance testing yet. Statistical synthesis (A7) and multi-annotator validation are future work.
